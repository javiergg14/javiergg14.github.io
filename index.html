Son of Ithaca

An action-adventure inspired by The Odyssey. You play as Telemachus, searching for his lost father, and you earn the powers of the gods through equippable masks that open up combat options and unlock the puzzles that block the way.

  • Nobody Games Studio
  • Wave Engine (C++ / OpenGL)
  • Windows

What I did

  • Designed Level 1: its puzzles and the distribution of its zones.
  • Placed the cameras in Level 1 to give it a more cinematic tone.
  • Programmed the enemy base that the game's enemies are built on.

Masks of the gods

Each mask is both a combat tool and the key to a kind of obstacle.

  • Hermes Float for a limited time and cross water.
  • Apollo Throw light projectiles that damage enemies and activate structures.
  • Ares Extra strength to push heavy boulders across the map.

20252D Metroidvania~16 people

The Apprentice

A story-driven Metroidvania in which you control a slime that can transform into animals it has met along the way. Lost in a forest twisted by magic, you gain new movement abilities and use them to explore, solve puzzles and fight bosses.

  • Pangolin Studios · CITM – UPC
  • C++
  • SDL
  • Box2D

What I did

  • Programmed the main character from start to finish in C++, using SDL and Box2D.
  • Movement kit: jump, double jump, glide, dash, wall slide, wall jump and pushing objects, each one switched on as the player unlocks it.
  • Animation state system for the player, with priorities between states so the right animation always wins.

About me

Making worlds that are fun to move through
